Visiting Drammen

Planning to visit for a booking or event? Here is a quick guide to staying, sightseeing, and getting to Drammen.

Hotels

  • Comfort Hotel Union Brygge – central, by the river and Union Scene, a few minutes' walk from the train station.
  • Quality Hotel River Station – right next to Drammen Station, with conference facilities.
  • Scandic Ambassadeur Drammen – by the waterfront, within walking distance of the station.
  • Clarion Collection Hotel Tollboden – central hotel with breakfast and evening buffet.
  • Forenom Serviced Apartments Drammen – apartment-style stays in Strømsø, near cafes and shops.

Places to see

  • Spiralen – a spiral tunnel road leading up to Spiraltoppen, with panoramic views over the city.
  • Elvepromenaden – the riverside promenade along Drammenselva, popular for walks and cycling.
  • Ypsilon Bridge – the iconic Y-shaped pedestrian bridge linking the city park and the library.
  • Bragernes Torg – one of the largest town squares in the Nordics, next to Bragernes Church.
  • Drammens Museum (Marienlyst) and Gulskogen Manor – art and cultural history museums.
  • Union Scene – a cultural venue for concerts and events in a converted industrial building.
  • Drammensbadet – Norway's largest swimming facility, with indoor and outdoor pools.

How to get here

  • By train: Drammen Station is a major hub, about 30–40 minutes from Oslo, with connections toward Bergen, Kristiansand, and Stavanger.
  • By car: Drammen lies on the E18 (Oslo–Kristiansand), where the E134 and Rv23 also meet, roughly 40 minutes from Oslo.
  • By bus: Frequent express bus connections link Drammen with Oslo and other Norwegian cities.
  • By air: The nearest airports are Oslo Airport Gardermoen and Sandefjord Torp Airport, both about an hour's drive away; Flytoget runs directly from Oslo Airport to Drammen.
